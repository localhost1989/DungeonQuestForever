# Changelog

## 3.52.0
- "How to find it" hints for 359 quests: where the cave or mine entrance is, which floor of a tower, which object to click (it is not an NPC), what an item drops from, what has to happen first for someone to appear. Shown under "now" in the quest and in its tip. Gathered from Warcraft Wiki, guides and player comments.
- A quest with nothing to count (find someone, talk to someone) keeps its hint while it is in your log; with no written hint, its own words say where ("in the caves beneath Sunnyglade").
- Quest chains: the next quest the same giver offers right after you hand one in is taken by itself, even when the game shows it grey.

## 3.51.1
- When the goals of the quest the arrow leads to are met, the arrow goes on to whom the quest is handed in (in a fight, as soon as it is over). Before, it was left empty and could be sent to the dungeon door.
- The arrow no longer goes to the dungeon door by itself while a quest is still to be handed in or finished out there, farther than 600 yards from the way in. A new Forever dungeon stands in the middle of a quest zone (Krol'dok Stronghold in Riverglades), and the quests around it counted as "by the dungeon".

## 3.51.0
- The way to every boss: inside a dungeon the game shows no map and no position, so under the next boss a line or two say how to walk to him from the last one. 164 bosses of the 18 classic dungeons have them.
- Where a goal is inside: a quest you have taken says where in the dungeon its goal lies ("near boss ...", "outside, before the portal", "all over the dungeon"), and with Dungeon Quest Atlas installed the spot is shown on its floor plan.
- Rewards in the journal: every quest page shows what it gives (the items to choose from, the items given, the money), with the item icons, a green arrow on the ones better than what you wear, and Shift-click into the chat. What the game itself shows at the quest giver or in your log is remembered and stands over the database.
- A new copy of a dungeon (another group, a reset) clears the killed bosses by itself as soon as the creatures around show it is another copy.
- Dire Maul: its wings are known as Dire Maul, so the panel no longer loses the dungeon inside.
- The last step of a chain that is only a hand-in in a city (the Platinum Discs to Thunder Bluff) is kept with its dungeon and gets its line and its road.
- Sunken Temple: the two dragons come after Jammal'an and Ogom, as they do in the game.
- Item names, colours and links are read through the game's newer item functions too, where the old one is missing.
- In a dungeon group, a short line about the addon goes once to each new group mate who has not got it (again after two hours, not more often than every 20 minutes). The settings menu turns it off.
- Usage statistics through Wago App (for those who turned them on there) now count every button and setting, so we can see what is used and what is not. No names, text or places are sent.

## 3.50.1
- Inside Uldaman, the quests done in the tunnels before the portal (the Tablet of Ryun'eh, the Garrett and Stoutarm chests) say "outside, before the portal": a click tells you to do them on the way out, and no longer sets the arrow to the entrance.

## 3.50.0
- A boss in the list shows your best item when it drops from him (up to two, from the best-gear lists for your class, talent tree and level), with its icon and name.
- Under the next boss, a short tip on how to fight him: 132 bosses have one.
- Alcaz Prison and Shaper's Terrace have their quests: 22 and 6, for the Horde and the Alliance, with the chains that lead to them (from Wowhead's WoW Forever database). Their names are in English for now.
- The header of a boss no longer counts "best in slot" items: the item itself is shown.

## 3.49.0
- Best gear: a Gear button on the panel (also /dq gear and the minimap menu) opens a window like the character frame. Every slot shows the best item for your class and talent tree at your level and the next steps (up to 60): the dungeon and boss that drop it, or the quest, vendor or craft that gives it; "equipped", "+N over equipped" or the level it needs. Hover for the item and where it comes from; the figure in the middle wears the set. The lists are those of Timesaver.gg (13 November 2026).
- A small gold sight at the end of a goal that is one creature (one to kill, or one thing only he drops): a click targets him, like /target. It is set out of combat and stays put in one.
- Boss kills are kept as long as you are in the same copy of the dungeon, known by the creatures in it: a wipe, a long run back or a /reload no longer forget them. Another copy (a reset, another group) starts afresh.
- Scarlet Monastery: the wing is known by its map from the first step, so the Cathedral shows the Cathedral's bosses alone.
- The plate on top leads to the nearest quest to hand in, also when it stands by the dungeon and something far is left.
- A dungeon put away (right click in the journal, or the menu behind the dots) gives way on the panel to the next one.
